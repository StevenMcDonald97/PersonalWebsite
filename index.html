Bio

I'm a senior software engineer and the UI/UX Technical Lead for HealthLevel, building clean, accessible, easy-to-use front ends for thousands of users. I currently head our data visualizations group, and I previously onboarded and managed an international team of 3 engineers. I also led our migration from Backbone to React, modernizing our front end stack and widening our hiring pool in the process. I have a bird's-eye view of the entire pipeline — from customer research through design to final code — and I know exactly where to jump in when I'm needed.

Paintings

When I'm not writing software, I'm painting — alla prima, oil on canvas, outdoors in one sitting.

I started taking art classes when I was just nine and my first I took my first serious painting instruction in high school, under Brigitte Curt and Jim Smyth. After finishing my CS degree at Cornell I spent a year in Florence studying at the Lorenzo de' Medici Institute and Chiaroscuro Studio. My favorite place to paint though has always been California, where I can chase the beautiful light and the soaring vistas of my home state.

Painting isn't a a separate life from my code. The palette this site is built on — ultramarine blue, cadmium orange, viridian green, hansa yellow — is the one on my easel, and judging an edge or a value is the same attention I bring to an interface.
